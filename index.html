Road Defect Reporting Portal (Form RD-7)

NOTICE: Scheduled maintenance every Wednesday 22:00-02:00. The portal may be unavailable. Enquiries lodged during this window must be re-submitted.

In terms of the Municipal Systems Act 32 of 2000 read together with the Roads and Stormwater By-laws (2014, as amended), members of the public may lodge an enquiry pertaining to a road surface defect ("pothole") within the municipal jurisdiction. Please note that the lodging of an enquiry does not constitute an undertaking by the Municipality to effect repairs within any specific timeframe. Enquiries are assessed in accordance with the Departmental Prioritisation Framework (DPF-2019) and attended to subject to budgetary availability and seasonal maintenance schedules.

Before proceeding, kindly ensure that you have the following information at hand, failing which your enquiry may be rejected: (a) the full street name and suburb; (b) the nearest cross street or intersection; (c) the applicable ward number (consult the Ward Lookup Register, available at any municipal customer care centre during office hours, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 15:30); (d) a photograph of the defect not exceeding 500KB in JPEG format; (e) your South African identity number or passport number for verification purposes.
